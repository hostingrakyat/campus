Mahasigma: College Simulator

## Short description (max 80)
Survive ghosting lecturers, tuition and course wars. Honors or dropout?

## Full description (max 4000)
Welcome to campus! In Mahasigma Simulator you're a freshman at an Indonesian university trying to make it to graduation: lecturers who leave you on read, seven-counter bureaucracy, tuition every semester, and parents who suddenly get laid off.

🎓 PICK YOUR MAJOR & STYLE
Start in Computer Science, Management or Medicine. Design your character in a cute 3D vinyl-toy style: hair, hijab, campus jacket, flannel shirt, all the way to the golden sigma hoodie.

📅 PLAN A WHOLE WEEK
Morning class or skip? Afternoon study or a part-time shift? Night out at the warkop or finishing assignments? Every choice changes your Energy, Mental health, Social life, grades and wallet. Random weekly news (public holidays, pop quizzes, power cuts) keeps every week different.

🎮 PLAYABLE ACTIVITIES
Don't just watch: catch the lecturer's key points, answer when you're called on, mash out a report before the deadline, keep your running rhythm, and pick the best reply in thesis meetings.

🗺️ FREE-ROAM CAMPUS
Use the joystick to walk around a very Indonesian campus: pick up dropped coins, hunt for a secret diamond, pet the orange cat, kick a ball into the goal and chat with other students.

📋 REAL CAMPUS BUREAUCRACY
Course registration wars, semester reports with GPA, tuition appeals, scholarships, leave semesters and tempting payday-loan apps. Part-time jobs can clash with your class schedule. And watch out for impulse-buying skins!

💬 STORIES & CUTSCENES
Dozens of events: lecturers out of town, the 7th title revision, the 13th-semester senior, the landlady knocking, calls from mom. Every choice has consequences.

🏆 10 DIFFERENT ENDINGS
Summa cum laude, cum laude, on-time graduation, the eternal 14th-semester student, switching majors, dropping out, and the Social Campus Balance. Look after your mental health: studying nonstop has consequences too.

🌏 TWO LANGUAGES
Indonesian and English, with optional subtitles in the other language.

Content note: the game touches on mental-health themes in a non-explicit way and points to support resources. If you're struggling, talk to someone you trust or a counseling service.

Free to play. Contains optional rewarded ads and in-app purchases (diamonds, cosmetics, remove ads).
